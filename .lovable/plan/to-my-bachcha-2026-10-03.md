# LETTER PAGE DESIGN

The letter should feel like a beautiful handwritten/printed personal letter rather than a normal website article.

* cream paper-like background

* very subtle paper grain/texture

* elegant serif typography for the letter

* handwritten-style accents for small personal elements

Do NOT overdo parallax or motion.

The animation should support the emotional writing rather than distract from it.
